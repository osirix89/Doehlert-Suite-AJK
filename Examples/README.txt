EXAMPLES AND VALIDATION DATASETS

- Example_01 to Example_06: synthetic datasets for practice and demonstration.
- Validation_01 to Validation_04: reference cases for exact hand calculations, a complete fit, lack of fit, and the 13-run minimum design.
- Doehlert_table1_Y1.csv and Doehlert_table1_Y2.csv: additional multiple-response datasets.

These files are for demonstration and testing only; they are not real experimental measurements. Import them from the application's File menu.
